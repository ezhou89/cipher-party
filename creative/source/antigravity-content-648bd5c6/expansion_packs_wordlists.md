# Cipher Party Expansion Packs — Deep Specification & 500-Card Master Catalog

This document provides an exhaustive, deep-dive specification for Cipher Party's **Pop Culture & Fandom Expansion Packs**:
* **Pack 1: Otaku Orbit** (Anime & Manga) — 100 Cards
* **Pack 2: Idol Wave** (K-Pop & Hallyu) — 100 Cards
* **Pack 3: Respawn** (Video Game Culture) — 100 Cards
* **Pack 4: Saturday Morning** (90s Kid Nostalgia) — 100 Cards
* **Pack 5: Multiverse** (Superheroes & Comics) — 100 Cards

**Total Expansion Cards:** 500 Cards  
**Total Combined Universe (Core 200 + Expansions 500):** 700 Unique Cards  

---

## 1. Mathematical Architecture & Campaign Capabilities

Expanding from 75 to **100 cards per pack** unlocks major gameplay thresholds:

| Format / Campaign | Cards Consumed | Pack Capacity (100 Cards) | Replayability Guarantee |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Standalone Best-of-3 Series** | 75 Cards | 75% of deck | **0 repeated cards across the entire series** |
| **Standalone Best-of-5 Series** | 100 Cards (or 125 if game 5) | ~90% of deck | Max 1-2 repeats in deciding game 5 |
| **Expanded 3-Team (5×6 Grid)** | 30 Cards / board | 3 full boards = 90 cards | 0 repeated cards |
| **Expanded 4-Team (6×6 Grid)** | 36 Cards / board | 2 full boards = 72 cards | 0 repeated cards |
| **Shuffled into Core (200 + 100 = 300 Cards)** | Variable | Sustains up to 12 back-to-back 5×5 boards | Total campaign freedom with zero repeats |

## 2. Hazard (Assassin) Profiles for Themed Play

The **Hazard card** is the single most important card on the board—if a team reveals it, they lose immediately (or are eliminated in 3-team/4-team mode). In themed packs, the best Hazard cards are words with high semantic gravity that spymasters will naturally hover near:

* **Otaku Orbit (Anime):** **`CURSE`**, **`OVERLORD`**, **`VOID`**, **`BETRAYAL`**.
  * *The Trap:* A spymaster clueing *"EVIL 3"* hoping their team picks `DEMON`, `VILLAIN`, and `ENEMY` might accidentally tempt their operatives into guessing the Hazard `CURSE` or `OVERLORD`.
* **Idol Wave (K-Pop):** **`SCANDAL`**, **`FAREWELL`**, **`HIATUS`**, **`BARRICADE`**.
  * *The Trap:* Clueing *"BREAK 2"* hoping for `COMEBACK` or `PRACTICE` risks the team picking `HIATUS` or `FAREWELL`.
* **Respawn (Video Games):** **`WIPEOUT`**, **`PERMADEATH`**, **`GLITCH`**, **`CRISIS`**.
  * *The Trap:* Clueing *"END 3"* for `BOSS`, `CHECKPOINT`, and `SCORE` risks hitting `PERMADEATH` or `WIPEOUT`.
* **Saturday Morning (90s Kid):** **`DETENTION`**, **`GROUNDED`**, **`REWIND`**, **`CLUELESS`**.
  * *The Trap:* Clueing *"PUNISHMENT 2"* for `PARENT` and `RECESS` risks hitting `GROUNDED` or `DETENTION`.
* **Multiverse (Comics):** **`DOOMSDAY`**, **`APOCALYPSE`**, **`BLACKOUT`**, **`NEMESIS`**.
  * *The Trap:* Clueing *"DESTRUCTION 3"* for `METEOR`, `TITAN`, and `INVASION` puts operatives directly in the blast radius of `DOOMSDAY`.
